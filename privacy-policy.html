Privacy Policy

Effective: November 8, 2026

Vellops is operated by MS ECOM LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through our website — including the AI chat on this site — and through the service we run for our business customers (a custom AI chatbot installed on the customer's own website, with lead alerts and follow-ups by email), and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Website chat logs — when you use the chat on vellops.com, we store the conversation: the messages you send and the assistant's replies, the date and time, the pages you chatted from, and any name, phone number, or email address you choose to share in the chat.
  • Phone numbers — if you choose to share one in the website chat, the contact form, or with our business directly.
  • Conversation content — the text of your website-chat conversations with our service, including questions you ask and booking details.
  • Contact-form details — the name, email address, phone number, and business name you submit through the contact form on this site.
  • Email correspondence — emails you send us, and emails the service sends (lead alerts, conversation summaries, booking confirmations, follow-ups), so we can operate the service and answer you.
  • Customer conversation records — for businesses that use our service, the website-chat conversations between the service and that business's website visitors, for the same reason: those records belong to the business (see "If you are a customer of a business that uses Vellops" below).

How we use it

  • To answer your questions in the website chat, and to follow up by email with the demo or information you asked for.
  • To email the business owner each new lead and conversation summary the chatbot captures.
  • To answer questions and book appointments in the website chat, using an AI assistant, and to send booking confirmations by email.
  • To produce reports for the business (for example, our Monday report: chats handled, leads captured, booked) and to improve the assistant's answer quality.
  • To respond to contact-form inquiries you send us.

We do not sell your personal information — including chat logs — and we never use conversation records to market anyone else's business to you. We share information only with the technology providers that help us deliver the service — for example, our hosting provider, the AI provider that generates chat replies, and our email provider for message delivery — and only as needed to operate the service.

If you are a customer of a business that uses Vellops

When a business puts our chatbot on its website, the conversations you have with that business's assistant are that business's records: the business owns them, sees every conversation, and can export them at any time. We process and store them only to run the service for that business. We never sell them, and we never use them to advertise to you.

Data retention and security

Website chat logs from vellops.com are kept for up to 12 months so we can follow up on conversations and improve the assistant, then deleted. Customer conversation records are kept while the business's account is active and for 30 days after cancellation (so the business can export them), then deleted — except records we must keep for legal or accounting purposes. Contact-form and email records are kept only as long as needed to operate the service and handle follow-ups.

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing mitchell@msecomllc.com. We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold, but no system is perfectly secure.

Website visitor identification

We use a visitor-identification service (RB2B) that helps us understand which businesses visit vellops.com. For visitors in the United States, the service may also identify the individual associated with a visit — such as a name, job title, employer, and business contact details — using cookies and similar technologies. We use this information only to follow up about our own services with businesses that have shown an interest in them.

The categories of personal information involved are identifiers, professional or employment-related information, and internet or network activity (such as the pages viewed on this site). We do not sell personal information for money.

Do Not Sell or Share (California residents). You may opt out of the sharing of your personal information for visitor-identification purposes at any time: email mitchell@msecomllc.com with the subject “Do Not Sell or Share”, or send the same request through the contact form on this site. We action every opt-out request and confirm it by reply.

Global Privacy Control. We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC): if your browser or extension sends a GPC signal, the visitor-identification script does not load on your visit.
